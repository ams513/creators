IIIHow you get paid

Commission: 50% of the sale price to start, rising as your sales grow (table below), after any discount and without sales tax, on each sale that comes through your own link, for Stark Homeroom, Stark Baseline and Stark Fellowship today. Only your link counts.

Paid by the store, automatically. Gumroad holds each sale 7 days, then pays you on your own schedule (bank account or PayPal) once your balance reaches $100 USD. That minimum is Gumroad's rule, not ours. You need a Gumroad account.

Fees are split in the same proportion as the commission, so you receive your rate of the price after the store's fees. A refund removes that commission. Your link credits a buyer for 30 days.

IVQuestions

Do you pay a sponsorship fee?

No. We cannot pay a sponsorship fee today. There is no flat payment and nothing paid per view. What we offer is a free licence, giveaway keys for your audience, and the commission above.

Do I have to say good things?

No. Say what you honestly think, good or bad. There is no script and we do not approve your video.

Do I have to tell my viewers?

Yes. A free licence and a commission are both things of value, and the FTC asks you to say so in the video itself, not only in the description (its guide).

What if sales are small?

Then the commission is small. We promise no amount. Gumroad pays out only once your balance reaches $100 USD, and a refund removes that sale's commission.

Which products carry a commission?

Today: Stark Homeroom, Stark Baseline and Stark Fellowship, which are on Gumroad. Every other product joins when it is listed there; until then it carries the free licence and keys only. The free licence and keys apply to every product, always.

Can I really run the trial on stream?

Yes. The trials run in a browser tab: no install, no account.

Does this mean you endorse me, or I endorse Stark?

No. A page we make for a creator is private and not indexed, and nothing here says anyone endorses anyone.

Do you track me?

No. This site has no trackers and no cookies, and our messages carry no open or click tracking.

How do I stop?

Reply no. We will not write again. You can end an affiliation yourself at any time; commission already earned stays yours.
